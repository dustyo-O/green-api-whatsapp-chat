### F1: Recovery can exceed the required 10 seconds
- severity: major
- where: src/chat/receive-loop.ts: request budgets and backOff
- problem: Without a browser `online` event, recovery can exceed functional §2.4’s 10-second limit. Both repeated stalled receives and stalled deletes violate it.
- evidence: After repeated failures, a stalled receive waits 8 seconds plus 5 seconds of backoff before polling again; a stalled delete alone can wait 15 seconds. The stalled-receive test covers only the first failure with a 1-second backoff, and no test covers a stalled delete during recovery.
- fix: Bound request timeout plus subsequent backoff below 10 seconds, leaving time for the next receive. Add recovery tests at maximum backoff and during a stalled delete, without emitting `online`.
